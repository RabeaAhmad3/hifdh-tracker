## Description

Track and support Quran memorization progress with Hifdh Tracker, built for the Qalam Institute Hifdh program.

**For Teachers:**
- Record daily assignments across new memorization (sabaq), recent review (sabqi), and long-term review (manzil)
- Log student behavior and classroom conduct
- Track attendance with late arrivals and excused absences
- View reports and analytics across your class
- Communicate directly with parents via real-time messaging
- Schedule parent-teacher meetings with built-in availability management

**For Parents:**
- Monitor your child's memorization progress and assignments
- View behavior reports and attendance history
- Access detailed progress reports with visual charts
- Message your child's teacher directly
- Book parent-teacher meetings at available time slots
- Receive push notifications for new assignments and messages

Hifdh Tracker brings teachers and parents together to support every student's Quran memorization journey.
